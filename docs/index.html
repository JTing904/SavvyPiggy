Why this one

Most apps track one pot. Your money has several.

🎯

One amount, every goal fed

Give each goal a percentage. A single deposit reaches all of them at once — no transferring money between pots by hand.

🪙

Cents are never invented

Every calculation runs in whole cents and rounds down; the odd leftover cent goes to the largest share. A split can never total more than you put in.

♻️

Finished goals step aside

Switch on overflow and a goal that hits its target stops taking a cut — its share is handed to the goals still short of theirs.

🏦

Borrowing stays honest

Money you borrow never lands in a goal. It is recorded as debt, and the next deposits clear it before anything is split.

📄

Your records, exportable

Any period exports as a PDF statement or a CSV you can open in a spreadsheet — yours to keep, whatever happens to the app.

🕯️

Overspending is allowed

Spend more than a goal holds and it simply goes negative. Nothing is quietly topped up from another goal behind your back.

How it works

Three steps, then it stays out of your way.

1

Redeem your invite code

Sign in with Google or an email address, then enter the code you were given. Each code works exactly once.

2

Create goals, set the shares

Name each goal, give it a target or leave it open-ended, then set the percentages until they add up to 100.

3

Put money in — or let a rule do it

Type one amount, or set a daily / weekly / monthly rule. Anything a rule missed while your phone was off is posted the next time you open the app.

Your data

What actually happens to your records.

No claims we cannot back up — here is the plain version, and the source code is public if you want to check it.

✓
It never touches your bank

SavvyPiggy asks for no card, no bank login, no account number. It records money you have already set aside yourself.

✓
Only your account can read your records

Everything is stored under your own user id in Google Firebase, behind rules that reject any request coming from another account.

✓
No ads, no analytics, no selling

There is no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK in the app, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

✓
Reminders stay on your phone

Every notification is scheduled by your own device. No push server ever sees that you are saving, or how much.

✓
You can take it all with you

Export any period as PDF or CSV whenever you like. Deleting old records never changes a single balance.

✓
Ask and it is deleted

Email the address in the footer and the whole account, with every record in it, is removed.

One thing this app is not: a bank. There is no deposit insurance, no interest, and no regulator behind it — it is a private record of your own saving habit.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Why do I need an invite code?

The app runs on a free database quota that every user shares. Invite codes keep the number of accounts small enough that it stays fast and stays free. Ask Edward for one.

Can I change the percentages later?

Any time, and it only affects deposits made from then on. Past records are never rewritten.

What if I spend more than a goal holds?

The goal goes negative and stays that way until future deposits bring it back. Nothing is moved from your other goals to hide it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No — and it never asks for banking details. You move the real money yourself; SavvyPiggy keeps the record.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It is built with web technology inside an Android shell, so iOS is possible later — but nothing is promised.

Why is it not on Google Play?

Publishing there costs a one-off developer fee and a review process. For now the APK is downloaded straight from GitHub, signed with the same key every release.

Does it cost anything?

No. No purchase, no subscription, no advert. It is a personal project, shared as it is.
