🐷 SavvyPiggy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026 · SavvyPiggy 1.0

SavvyPiggy is a personal savings tracker made by one person. It is free, carries no advertising, and makes no money from you or your data. This page explains exactly what it stores and how to have it removed.

Short version: SavvyPiggy never connects to a bank and never asks for payment details. What you type into the app is stored under your own account in Google Firebase, readable only by you, and deleted whenever you ask.

1. What is collected

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with an email address instead, only that address and your chosen display name are stored.
  • What you enter: your goals and their names, targets and icons; the amounts you deposit, spend or borrow; notes you attach to them; automatic deposit rules; and your notification settings.
  • Goal photos: if you add a picture to a goal, it is shrunk and stored with that goal.
  • Your invite code: recorded once, so a code cannot be used twice.

2. What is never collected

  • No bank credentials, card numbers or account numbers — the app has no way to move real money.
  • No location, no contacts, no photo library access beyond a picture you pick yourself.
  • No advertising identifiers and no analytics: the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK at all.

3. Where it is stored

Records are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google on servers outside your country. Access rules on that database reject any read or write that does not come from your own signed-in account. The developer does not browse individual accounts, and there is no admin screen that shows other people's savings.

4. Notifications

Reminders and milestone alerts are scheduled by your own phone, using Android's alarm system. They are not sent from a server, so nothing about your saving leaves the device in order to notify you. Turning them off in the app removes the alarms from the phone.

5. Exports

PDF statements and CSV files are generated on your phone and handed to whatever app you choose to share them with. They are not uploaded anywhere by SavvyPiggy.

6. How long it is kept

Records stay until you remove them. Inside the app you can clear ledger entries older than twelve months yourself — that removes the history, never the balances. Notification cards inside the app are cleared automatically after ninety days.

7. Deleting your account

Email kengtingtan@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for deletion. Your account and every record under it will be removed. Export anything you want to keep first — deletion cannot be undone.

8. Children

SavvyPiggy is not aimed at children under 13 and is handed out by invitation only.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous wording stays visible in the project's public history on GitHub.
